# Q2 Planning

## Summary

Quarterly planning session for Digital Collections. Team agreed to
prioritize the upload backlog with a target of 500 items by June 30.

## Key Points

The upload workflow bottleneck was identified as metadata review, not the
upload tooling itself. A dedicated metadata sprint is planned for May. Three
staff members will receive cataloging-tool training before the sprint.
